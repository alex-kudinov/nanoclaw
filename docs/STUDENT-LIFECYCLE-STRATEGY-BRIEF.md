# Astra strategy brief: durable student lifecycle

Version 1, 2026-09-07. Owner requested strategy and plan, with CoachGrader-style
model routing adopted first. This brief authorizes document assembly and review;
it is not production promotion authority. `.program/state.json` remains the
only execution portfolio. Existing charter/outcomes/boundaries remain in force.

## Outcome

Every valid commercial or grant promise must become durable, attributable work:
what was promised, under which terms, to whom, for which dated class if any,
which systems must reflect it, and which evidence proves completion. Partial
failure must preserve the promise and capacity commitment while retaining an
owned exception. Repeated events or installments must not create extra students,
orders, enrollments or seats.

The immediate supervision repair is complete. The name-independent supervision
bridge is a narrow first migration, not the completed lifecycle. At the recorded
audit snapshot there are 47 local checkout entries / 37 active; the entitlement
catalog has eight offers and only one active checkout is on the new bridge.
These are source-snapshot coverage figures, not verified current provider totals.

No recursive Codex delegation, silent Astra fallback or global setting changes.
One program claim covers the slice and its worker; do not confuse a two-child
runtime ceiling with authorization for two concurrent program claims/releases.

Use the observed CoachGrader policy and active pilot as design provenance.
Do not claim its pilot is finished or its cost savings measured. Explicit Sol
dispatch is the demonstrated mechanism; project-default behavior in a fresh
trusted task requires separate observation and must be labeled if unverified.

## Strategic choices

1. Extend the existing offer/bundle/component foundation and Company OS ledger.
   No parallel student master in Sheets, Plutio, Heartbeat or CoachGrader.
2. Distinguish conceptual entities. A program, commercial offer, payment plan,
   entitlement bundle, course/access group and dated class are different things.
   Different IDs are normal; undocumented relationships are the failure mode.
3. Make the cross-system identity registry typed, scoped, versioned and
   source-bound. Existing offer keys remain stable. Never repurpose historical
   keys, delete old aliases to hide drift or revise historical enrollment terms.
4. Retain domain authority. Courses/accepted program facts own curriculum and
   inclusions; accepted commercial/checkout sources own offered terms; providers
   own native payments, access and progress; accepted schedule sources own dates;
   Company OS owns reconciled enrollment, assignments and work/receipt state.
   A coordinated publication workflow assembles pinned exports and checks them.
   It does not silently move price or curriculum authority to another repository.
5. Separate accepting a business event, recording canonical state, and applying
   provider projections. Independent accounting must continue when registration
   is held. Canonical class commitments cannot depend on a successful roster API.
6. Migrate bounded populations progressively. Use explicit temporary legacy
   dispositions and a coverage ratchet; do not switch all 37 active offers at once.

Preserve accepted capacity decision
`decision-academy-capacity-simple-sync-strategy-2026-09-06`: committed-seat
accounting, daily reconciliation, threshold-triggered cached website status,
targeted cache refresh and operator adjustment. The owner explicitly replaced
synchronous checkout reservations and short-lived holds, accepting the small
residual race risk. This plan must not reintroduce those mechanisms or a live
NanoClaw dependency in checkout through an older design or completion statement.

## Identity contract requirements

Maintain program keys, offer keys/versions, bundle/component keys/versions,
provider scopes/object types/IDs, commercial-agreement/order references,
participant/seat/enrollment identities and dated delivery-block/pool identities.
Store provenance, effective dates, status, owner, evidence and resolution rules.

Provider bindings are typed relationships, not a universal one-ID-to-one-offer
table. Several products/prices can represent an offer; a reused native product
may need price plus explicit checkout/contract context to select one commercial
offer. A shared Heartbeat group can support several entitlements and must never
be reverse-mapped into a purchase. Ambiguous bindings are held, not guessed.

Keep payment-plan semantics explicit. One installment agreement is one order
with several obligations/receipts, not one new order per installment PI. The
current local funding contract supports fully settled receipts only; installment,
refund/dispute, sponsor and service-consumption behavior must be proved in their
own admitted slices before broadening the new production writer. Canonical
funding IDs prevent delivery duplicates; agreement/order links prevent different
payments on one plan from multiplying enrollment. Neither payer identity nor
an amount match establishes participant identity or an offer.

Every offer has an explicit treatment: enrollment, component/service fulfillment,
non-student sale, grant/free access, retired/historical, or unresolved. Do not
force delivered supervision sessions into the supervision-training student roster.
Preserve purchased service allowances and consumption separately from class seats.

## Publication and management workflow

Draft a new offer/variant or change, reconcile source-owned terms and native
bindings, validate its required destinations, then publish an immutable version.
Changing business promises needs the relevant owner decision. Routine labels can
change without changing identity. Existing native IDs need not be renamed.

Before a new or changed offer goes live, check all sale modes/locales, catalog
coverage, account-scoped payment identities, fulfillment treatment, required
provider projections, schedule/pool compatibility where applicable and readback.
Use generated projections where feasible and deterministic drift checks elsewhere.
During migration, explicitly grandfather unchanged legacy entries with evidence
and owner; fail new gaps or regressions. Turn full strict coverage on only after
the existing gaps are reconciled. A legacy allowlist must not silently grow.

Start with version-controlled manifests and a documented operator change process.
A later administrative screen can propose/validate/publish those same records;
it must not become a second editable master. Do not build a catalog UI first.

## Sequence and phase exit criteria

This is a dependency design, not a second current queue. The final plan must map
each phase to exact existing canonical work items or an explicit proposed delta.

0. Adopt routing and complete this strategy/plan. Exit: actual Sol dispatch,
   local policy/config installation evidence, independent review and no authority
   expansion. No lifecycle application implementation in this task.
1. Reconcile the complete catalog and source relationships. Begin read-only.
   Inventory website variants/locales, both Stripe accounts, Plutio item/contract
   references, Heartbeat access/course mappings, roster, curriculum and schedules.
   Resolve MCS dated-slug/mcs-full differences rather than treating absence as
   deletion. Exit: every selected active item has a source-bound disposition;
   unresolved business choices are explicit and block only affected promotion.
2. Establish the common publication contract and checks. Exit: new/changed offers
   cannot publish with missing bindings, and pinned consumer exports reproduce
   without manual parallel edits. One accepted offer update is the first proof.
3. Prove lifecycle event/finance semantics and native writer integration. Extend
   rather than rewrite the existing local admission/store foundations. Exit:
   provider-authenticated inputs, participant evidence, agreement/receipt identity,
   exactly one legacy/new writer owner, accounting continuity, transaction/replay
   safety and scoped projection outbox all pass independent/disposable proofs.
   Prepare and independently verify the selected provider projection adapters,
   destination readback, uncertain-acceptance recovery and rollback locally before
   any new paid event can be diverted to the new writer. A separate local
   `work:student-enrollment-projection-foundation` package owns that readiness.
4. Pilot an exact eligible population. Recommended first: new, fully settled,
   self-purchased supervision tuition with an explicitly mapped dated class.
   This recommendation is not activation authority. Confirm the class capacity
   and participant evidence first. Ma. Carmen's repaired legacy event is reference
   evidence, not permission to run it through the new writer a second time.
   Canonical admission and verified provider delivery are distinct evidence
   milestones within one explicitly authorized bounded rollout. Projection
   permissions are separately explicit, never inferred from canonical authority;
   both paths must be ready before routing real events. Do not intentionally
   leave a newly paid participant awaiting access/roster delivery until another
   project phase. If the projection path cannot be enabled in the approved
   rollout, do not divert that population from its established fulfillment path.
   Full end-to-end supervision acceptance precedes unrelated finance-family
   expansion; phase 6's continuing reconciliation is not permission to defer
   initial fulfillment. Register additional candidate scope when necessary;
   a candidate-count review signal is not a reason to hide missing preparation.
5. Expand to payment plans and additional funding/provider/product families only
   after their semantics and aliases are reconciled. Backfill historical records
   using a bounded dry-run, no-duplicate comparisons, ambiguity holds and verified
   per-destination readback. Never infer reassignment from a payment date.
6. Reconcile continuously and retire legacy paths by proven population coverage.
   Check missed events, accepted payments without completion, missing projections,
   orphaned/conflicting aliases, stale exceptions and capacity discrepancies.
   Exit: no unowned gaps in the admitted population and observed natural outcomes;
   elapsed time, a webhook 200 or a deployment receipt cannot establish success.
7. Extend trustworthy state through participation, learning progress, assessment,
   completion and the separately authorized service/certificate/marketing actions.
   Reuse the existing lifecycle action-catalog/runtime/minion work items rather
   than creating another queue. Heartbeat access is not course progress; progress
   needs documented API/webhook evidence and stays unavailable if unsupported.
   CoachGrader contributes assessment evidence, not commercial enrollment or a
   new student master. Preserve the current grading, certificate-approval,
   consent/suppression and service-versus-marketing boundaries. Exit criteria
   must include explicit NO_ACTION and unavailable-evidence cases, not only sends.

## Supervision pilot acceptance

Exactly one source-bound commercial episode and participant enrollment; correct
frozen promises and financial agreement; exact dated supervision class; one seat
commitment/assignment; required roster/access targets verified; accounting intact.
Duplicate events/aliases/retries create no second effect. A provider projection
failure leaves the funded promise counted and an owned recoverable exception.
An over-capacity payment remains accounted for and visible for owner resolution;
it is not discarded or automatically assigned beyond approved capacity.

Prove negative/failure paths with synthetic/disposable tests; do not manufacture
customer payments, disable real provider services or send customer messages just
to obtain a canary. Require a real authorized new-event readback when one occurs.
Natural observation still pending must stay pending, not force a fake completion.

## Decisions and unresolved boundaries

- Exact supervision class capacity and authoritative participant roster.
- Which payment-plan state grants/holds entitlement and a class commitment;
  subsequent installments, cancellation, arrears, refunds and disputes.
- Sponsor/multi-seat participant confirmation, transfers and service allowances.
- Ambiguous shared native IDs, historical aliases and MCS offer/class relationships.
- Exact production population, migrations 146/147/store promotion, both real writer
  paths, projection permissions, rollback and observation criteria.
- Reconciliation cadence, owner notification route and aging/service targets.

List each decision with owner, evidence required, affected phase and safe holding
behavior. Do not ask the user to decide technical details we can resolve from
sources, and do not infer any of the above merely to make a plan look complete.

## Evidence and release discipline

Every slice receives a bounded packet, immutable source lineage, file/resource
ownership, positive/negative acceptance, independent review, correction receipts
and exact deployment authority. One integration/release writer revalidates the
current live commit and all intervening releases before activation. Concurrent
Sales NC-20260907-002 started from aa73538c; supervision bridge 138e43ab must not
be lost by deploying that older ancestry. The strategy branch starts at final
closure c9c65dfb; the newer enrollment/admission foundations live on another
reviewed but undeployed branch and require deliberate integration.

Track coverage by source/offer/variant, canonical orders without completion,
unverified provider projections, duplicate suppression, exception ownership/age,
class-capacity variance, natural readbacks and rollback readiness. Propose targets
after baseline measurement; no fabricated success rates or model cost savings.
