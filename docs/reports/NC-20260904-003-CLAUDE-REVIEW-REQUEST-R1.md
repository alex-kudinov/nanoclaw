## AAMC guideline requirements

Full-graduation evidence must include organization name, AAMC program name, participant name, participant contact hours, confirmation of all AAMC requirements, graduation date, authorized signature, and completed ICF evaluation-tool training with applicable levels. AAMC logo is optional and may be used only for a full graduate.

Partial-completion evidence must include organization name, completed module/class, participant name, participant contact hours, completion date, a statement that it is a portion of the named full AAMC program, and an authorized signature. It must not use the AAMC logo or imply graduation.
