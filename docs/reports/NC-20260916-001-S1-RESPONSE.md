No obligation in this delta warrants DEFER or OWNER: each proposed KEEP is required to make the existing pending refund job resolve and to prevent Payment Log from overstating retained revenue, and each has a bound, falsifiable proof already specified in the request. There is no ambiguous ownership question raised in the delta — Adyen/WordPress remain money-movement authority, and NanoClaw's role is confined to receipt, projection and status update within its existing owned surfaces.

## Largest avoidable burden

Treating refunds as a second payment pipeline — i.e., a parallel route, recorder, retry mechanism, or roster-aware path mirroring the payment flow. This would duplicate operational ownership and create surface area (new queue/worker/credential) explicitly excluded by the constraints.

## Smaller path

One branch in the existing signed receiver for `REFUND success`, one additive PII-free Postgres table (migration 170) keyed by refund PSP and linked to the original projected payment, one Payment Log status-cell update (never touching amount/provider ID/learner/date), and an explicit no-op on Student Roster. No new service, endpoint, credential, queue, worker, scheduler, or customer communication.
