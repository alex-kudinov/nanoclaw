### MCS Program (Mentor Coach Specialization)
- **Full name:** ICF Mentor Coach Specialization
- **URL:** /mcs
- **Certification type:** ICF Mentor Coach Specialization (MCS) credential — issued at three levels: MCS-ACC, MCS-PCC, MCS-MCC
- **Effective date:** Required January 1, 2027 for all mentor coaches serving ICF credential candidates
- **What it is:** A new individual specialization from ICF that formally recognizes coaches qualified to provide mentor coaching to ICF credential candidates at a specific level
- **Two paths to earn MCS:**
  - **Standard Path:** Minimum 41 hours of MC-aligned education (≥50% synchronous). Tandem's Mentor Coach Training ($2,997, 71 hours, AAMC-accredited) satisfies this path.
  - **Credit for Prior Learning (CPL):** Minimum 10 hours of MC-aligned education + evidence of prior practice (5 credentialed mentees in 3 years or attestation letter). Tandem's Mentor Coaching Foundations ($299, 10 hours) satisfies this path.
- **ICF application fee:** $50 (member) / $125 (non-member) through March 31, 2027; $125 / $200 after. One fee covers all levels applied for simultaneously.
- **Evaluation training required per level:** ACC BARS (for MCS-ACC), PCC Markers (for MCS-PCC), MCC BARS (for MCS-MCC) — obtained from ICF directly for CPL path; embedded in Mentor Coach Training for Standard Path
- **Renewal:** Every 3 years — 10 hours MC-aligned continuing education + active credential + $100/$200 fee
- **Prerequisites:** Must hold an active ICF credential (ACC renewed at least once, PCC, or MCC)
- **Key differentiators:**
  - Tandem offers both preparation paths: MCT for Standard Path, MCF for CPL
  - Both built by Cherie Silas, MCC and Alex Kudinov, MCC
  - Tandem holds AAMC accreditation (granted July 1, 2026) for the Standard Path program
  - Cherie and Alex hold MCS at all three levels

**IMPORTANT — MCS is a credential designation, not a standalone training product.** Coaches earn MCS through preparation (Standard Path or CPL) plus ICF application. Tandem's two preparation programs are Mentor Coach Training ($2,997) and Mentor Coaching Foundations ($299). Do not quote the old $5,000/$3,500 pricing — that has been superseded.

## ICF Credentialing & Exam Information

### ICF Credential Requirements (Summary)

**ACC (Associate Certified Coach):**
- 60+ hours of coach-specific education
- 100+ hours of coaching experience (at least 75 paid; remaining 25 may be pro bono)
- 10+ hours of mentor coaching
- Pass the ICF ACC Exam
- Performance evaluation

**PCC (Professional Certified Coach):**
- 125+ hours of coach-specific education
- 500+ hours of coaching experience (at least 450 paid, minimum 25 clients over 24+ months)
- 10+ hours of mentor coaching
- Pass the ICF Credentialing Exam (PCC/MCC)
- Performance evaluation (inside the program on the Level 2 path; 2 recordings + transcripts on the Portfolio path)

**MCC (Master Certified Coach):**
- 200+ hours of coach-specific education
- 2,500+ hours of coaching experience
- 10+ hours of mentor coaching
- Pass the ICF Credentialing Exam (PCC/MCC)
- Performance evaluation

### ICF Credentialing Exam

ICF uses two separate exams: the **ACC Exam** for ACC candidates and the **ICF Credentialing Exam** for PCC and MCC candidates.

**ACC Exam:**
- **Format:** Computer-based, multiple-choice (coaching scenarios where you choose the best ICF-aligned response)
- **Administrator:** Administered by Pearson VUE on behalf of ICF
- **Content:** Based on ICF Core Competencies, coaching ethics, and the definition and boundaries of professional coaching
- **Questions:** 60 multiple-choice questions
- **Scoring:** Scaled scoring with a passing score of 460
- **Time limit:** 90 minutes including an optional break
- **Availability:** Can be taken at Pearson VUE test centers worldwide or via online proctoring
- **Cost:** $275 (separate from ICF credential application fee)
- **Confirm details:** ICF sets these details and can change them — confirm on the [current ICF ACC Exam page](https://coachingfederation.org/credentialing/icf-credential-exams/acc-exam/) before sitting it

**ICF Credentialing Exam (PCC/MCC):**
- **Format:** Computer-based, situational judgment format (select the best and worst action for each coaching scenario)
- **Administrator:** Administered by Pearson VUE on behalf of ICF
- **Content:** Based on ICF Core Competencies, coaching ethics, and the definition and boundaries of professional coaching
- **Questions:** 78 situational-judgment items
- **Scoring:** Scaled scoring with a passing score of 460
- **Time limit:** 180 minutes including an optional break
- **Same exam for PCC and MCC:** The same ICF Credentialing Exam is used for both PCC and MCC credentials — there is no separate MCC-level exam
- **Availability:** Can be taken at Pearson VUE test centers worldwide or via online proctoring
- **Confirm details:** ICF sets these details and can change them — confirm on the [current ICF PCC/MCC Exam page](https://coachingfederation.org/credentialing/icf-credential-exams/pcc-mcc-exam/) before sitting it

**General exam notes:**
- **Preparation:** Tandem programs include free exam prep courses as part of the curriculum (individual coaching prep for ACC; both individual and team coaching prep for PCC/ACTC)
- **Retake policy:** Can be retaken if not passed; ICF sets waiting period between attempts

### Program & Enrollment

**Q: What's the difference between ACC and PCC programs?**
A: ACC (Level 1) provides 60+ hours and prepares for the ACC credential (100 coaching hours required). The Systems Coach bridge (Level 2) provides 65+ additional hours and prepares for PCC + ACTC dual credentials (500 coaching hours required for PCC). The Systems Coach bridge requires 60+ hours of prior ICF-accredited training. Both are $3,999 for the full program. You can enter PCC directly without earning ACC first if you have 60+ hours of prior training from another provider.

**Q: Can I start with ACC and upgrade to PCC later?**
A: Yes. ACC graduates can continue to PCC through the Systems Coach bridge program ($3,999). Alternatively, the Professional Coach Program ($7,499) bundles ACC + PCC + ACTC in one enrollment, saving $499 vs. buying separately.

**Q: Are programs fully online?**
A: Yes. All programs are delivered live online via Zoom. The ACC program is a hybrid format — self-paced online learning modules PLUS live interactive Zoom sessions. They are NOT self-paced recordings only — classes include live, interactive sessions with real-time instruction and practice.

**Q: What are the payment plan options?**
A: All programs offer module-by-module enrollment as the primary flexible option — individual modules start at $399 each, and Coaching Foundations is free. For the full program, installment plans are available when explicitly requested. Contact admissions for specific payment schedules.

**Q: Do you offer scholarships or discounts?**
A: Some programs offer founding pricing with limited seats per cohort. The Professional Coach Program ($7,499) saves $499 vs. enrolling in ACC and Systems Coach separately. Contact admissions about current availability.

**Q: Does my prior coaching training count toward Tandem programs?**
A: The key question is whether your prior studies are ICF-accredited and include CCEUs. Do not assume a prior diploma is ICF-accredited just because a lead states it is — always ask for the graduation certificate before advising on credit transfer or path eligibility. If the training is not ICF-accredited, the path is portfolio paperwork or starting from scratch; non-accredited hours either don't transfer or transfer at a reduced rate (roughly 1 hour per 3 completed). Do NOT speculate about credit for accredited training — that requires further evaluation. Ask the lead whether their prior training is ICF-accredited and has CCEUs, then explain the fallback path.

**Q: When do cohorts start?**
A: ACC cohorts start in the first week of the month. Systems Coach (PCC) modules run on a flexible schedule with multiple entry points. Do not state specific mid-month dates. If you do not have a confirmed upcoming cohort date, say "cohorts run monthly — the next one starts in early [month]."

**Q: Is there a faster path through the program?**
A: Nothing prevents students from taking multiple modules simultaneously (attending multiple classes per week) to compress the timeline. Doubling up during Module 1 is not recommended — it lays the foundation — but after Module 1, students can run modules in parallel. Note that two mandatory constraints set the floor on overall timeline regardless of module pace: (1) mentor coaching requires 10 hours spread over a minimum of 3 months per ICF rules; (2) the ACC credential requires 100 coaching practice hours, which typically takes longer than the coursework itself. New cohorts start regularly, providing flexibility to stack modules.

**Q: How do coaches accumulate the 100 practice hours for ACC?**
A: Most students use Reciprocoach — a platform where coaches exchange sessions with each other for a nominal fee — to build their hours alongside others in the same position. This is the most practical path for new coaches who don't yet have a full client roster.

**Q: Where is Tandem's ICF accreditation number?**
A: ICF does not issue accreditation numbers. Programs are accredited as Level 1 or Level 2 and listed publicly on the ICF website. To verify Tandem's accreditation, search the ICF program directory at coachingfederation.org.

**Q: How much does the full ACC certification cost (all-in)?**
A: Tandem's full ACC Level 1 program is $3,999. Add ICF credential application fee ($175 member / $325 non-member), ICF credentialing exam fee ($275), and optional ICF membership ($245/yr). Total investment is approximately $4,200–$4,700 depending on membership status. Individual modules start at $399 each, and the Coaching Foundations module is free.

**Q: Can I try before I commit?**
A: Yes. The Coaching Foundations module (ICF Competencies & Ethics) is completely free — no credit card, no commitment. For the Systems Coach (PCC) program, two modules are free. Experience the teaching style and community before investing.

### ICF Credentials

**Q: Do I need coaching experience before enrolling?**
A: No prior coaching experience is required to enroll. However, you will need coaching hours (100 for ACC, 500 for PCC) to apply for the ICF credential after completing the program.

**Q: Does the program include mentor coaching?**
A: Yes. ACC and PCC programs include the required 10 hours of mentor coaching. This is integrated into the program — no separate purchase needed. For Level 1 (ACC) programs, mentor coaching is positioned after training completion, not concurrent with it — students get more value from mentor coaching feedback once they have the foundational skills from training.

**Q: How long does it take to get certified?**
A: Program completion typically takes 6–12 months. Total time to ICF credential depends on accumulating the required coaching hours, which varies by individual.

**Q: What's the difference between Tandem's programs and ICF certification?**
A: Tandem provides the ICF-accredited training. ICF grants the credential. After completing a Tandem program, graduates apply directly to ICF for their credential (ACC, PCC, etc.).

**Q: Do ICF hours from prior non-ICF experience count?**
A: No. ICF coaching hours start accumulating from the first ICF-accredited class. Prior non-ICF coaching experience does not partially cover the requirement.

**Q: Is there a separate exam for MCC?**
A: No. PCC and MCC candidates take the same ICF Credentialing Exam (78 situational-judgment items, 180 minutes). There is no separate MCC-level exam. However, ACC candidates take a different, separate ACC Exam (60 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes). MCC BARS follows the same structure and content as PCC Markers, so prior PCC Markers work transfers.

**Q: How does MCS differ from the ICF Coaching Supervision Specialization?**
A: They are completely separate tracks. The MCS (Mentor Coach Specialization) is Tandem's ICF-accredited program for coaches who mentor other coaches. The CSS (Coaching Supervisor Specialization) is ICF's individual designation for coaching *supervisors* — eligibility is an active PCC or MCC plus 41+ hours of supervision training (≥50% synchronous), against ICF's 8 Coaching Supervision Competencies (published September 2024); valid 3 years. Tandem's program for that pathway is **Coaching Supervision Mastery**, ICF-accredited under AACS and enrolling now (October 7, 2026 – February 10, 2027).

### Mentor Coaching & MCS

**Q: What's the difference between MCS, Mentor Coach Training, and Mentor Coaching Foundations?**
A: Three distinct offerings:
- **MCS (Mentor Coach Specialization):** An ICF credential designation (not a Tandem product) — earned through preparation via either path below, plus ICF application ($50–$125). Required January 1, 2027 for all mentor coaches serving credential candidates.
- **Mentor Coach Training / MCS Standard Path:** AAMC-accredited live cohort program (71 hours, $2,997, payable in 3×$999). Satisfies the Standard Path to MCS. 12 weekly live classes, 9-student cohort cap. Includes Foundations as pre-work, embedded ACC BARS + PCC Markers from the ICF Facilitator Guide, 5 observed sessions, two-part capstone. Led by Cherie Silas, MCC across all 12 classes.
- **Mentor Coaching Foundations:** Self-paced CCE program (10 hours, $299 launch / $397 regular). Satisfies the CPL (Credit for Prior Learning) path education requirement. Entry-level for experienced mentor coaches who can document prior practice.

**Q: Do I need Mentor Coaching Foundations before MCS or Mentor Coach Training?**
A: No. Foundations is an optional entry point for the CPL path. Standard Path candidates should enroll directly in Mentor Coach Training ($2,997) — Foundations is included as just-in-time pre-work. However, if you complete Foundations first and later enroll in the live cohort, you may receive credit for overlapping content.

**Q: What is AAMC?**
A: AAMC stands for Advanced Accreditation in Mentor Coaching. It is an ICF accreditation that organizations hold to deliver Standard Path mentor coaching programs. ICF published the AAMC standards on May 27, 2026 and opened applications in June 2026. Tandem Coaching Academy was granted the AAMC on July 1, 2026 (valid through July 2029) — the first and only AAMC-accredited program in North America.

**Q: Does MCS-PCC cover mentoring ACC candidates?**
A: No. MCS credentials are strictly per-level: MCS-PCC does NOT cover mentoring ACC candidates. Coaches who want to mentor at both ACC and PCC levels need MCS-ACC and MCS-PCC separately, each with their respective evaluation training (ACC BARS for MCS-ACC, PCC Markers for MCS-PCC), obtained from ICF directly. One safe harbor applies: ACC candidates may be mentored by either an MCS-ACC or an MCS-PCC holder.

**Q: What are the MCS CPL path documentation criteria?**
A: Documentation criteria depend on how the coach mentored. If mentoring was through a school or program, the school writes a letter and mentees do not need to have achieved ICF credentials. If mentoring was done independently, mentees must have achieved ICF credentials after the mentorship, and ACC renewal hours do not count. Graded assessments in Mentor Coaching Foundations are a mix of quizzes and written assignments per module — not all instructor-graded written submissions.

**Q: Do I need clients for mentor coaching or supervision?**
A: It depends on the format:
- **Individual mentor coaching:** Yes — requires actual coaching clients. You receive feedback on your real coaching sessions.
- **Coaching supervision:** Yes — requires actual coaching clients. It is reflective practice about your actual work as a coach.
- **Group mentoring:** No — uses peer practice in a classroom setting. Participants coach peers and receive feedback to develop at their target certification level.

**Q: Can I buy BARS or Markers evaluation training separately?**
A: No. As of June 2026, all Evaluation Training products (ACC BARS, PCC Markers, MCC BARS) have been discontinued as standalone purchases per ICF guidance. Standard Path students (Mentor Coach Training, $2,997) receive ACC BARS and PCC Markers embedded in the program, delivered from the ICF Facilitator Guide. CPL-path students (Mentor Coaching Foundations, $299) obtain BARS/Markers from ICF directly. MCC-credentialed enrollees in MCT receive MCC BARS as a free self-paced bonus (outside the 71 AAMC hours).

### Common Misconceptions to Correct
- "ICF certification" → ICF grants *credentials*, not certifications. Tandem's programs are *ICF-accredited training*.
- "Online means self-paced" → Tandem's programs are LIVE online (with self-paced components in ACC and ACTC), not recordings only.
- "I need ACC before PCC" → Not required. Can enter PCC directly.
- "MCS and Mentor Coach Training are the same" → Different things. MCS is an ICF credential designation earned through preparation (Standard Path or CPL) plus application. Mentor Coach Training ($2,997) is Tandem's AAMC-accredited program that satisfies the Standard Path. Mentor Coaching Foundations ($299) satisfies the CPL path.
- "Evaluation Training is available for purchase" → ALL Evaluation Training products are discontinued and cannot be sold separately per ICF guidance. Standard Path students receive them embedded in MCT; CPL-path coaches obtain them from ICF directly.
- "I can buy BARS/Markers training separately" → No. Standard Path students get them embedded; CPL-path coaches get them from ICF.
- "All Tandem instructors are MCCs" → Not accurate. ACC instructors include both MCCs and PCCs. Represent credentials accurately.
- "I don't need clients for mentor coaching" → Individual mentor coaching and supervision require actual coaching clients. Only group mentoring uses peer practice.
- "Tandem has an ICF accreditation number" → ICF does not issue accreditation numbers. Programs are listed in the ICF program directory at coachingfederation.org.
- "Prior experience speeds up the program" → The pace is the same for everyone regardless of background. ICF hours start from the first ICF-accredited class.
- "Class recordings are available" → Tandem does not keep individual class recordings. Students access class materials through the community platform.
- "MCS-PCC covers ACC-level mentoring" → MCS is strictly per-level. MCS-PCC covers PCC-candidate mentoring only (safe harbor: MCS-PCC holders may also mentor ACC candidates). MCS-ACC is required otherwise for ACC-candidate mentoring.
- "Tandem provides BARS/Markers for CPL-path coaches" → CPL-path coaches obtain evaluation training from ICF directly. Only Standard Path/MCT students receive it embedded in the program.
- "Systems Coach modules must be taken in order" → Modules are drop-in and can be taken in any order.
- "The same exam is used for ACC and PCC" → No. ICF now uses two separate exams: the ACC Exam (60 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes) for ACC candidates, and the ICF Credentialing Exam (78 situational-judgment items, 180 minutes) for PCC and MCC candidates. Both have a passing score of 460.
- "There's a separate MCC exam" → There is no separate MCC-level exam. PCC and MCC share the ICF Credentialing Exam. MCC BARS follows the same structure as PCC Markers.
- "My prior training must be ICF-accredited because I said so" → Do not assume. Always ask for the graduation certificate before advising on credit transfer or path eligibility. Non-accredited hours either don't transfer or transfer at a reduced rate (roughly 1 hour per 3 completed).
- "ACTC and AATC are the same thing" → AATC (Advanced Accreditation in Team Coaching) is the organizational accreditation that Tandem holds from ICF for its team coaching curriculum. ACTC (Advanced Certification in Team Coaching) is the individual credential ICF awards to coaches who meet its own experience, assessment, and application requirements. Tandem's graduation certificate carries the AATC seal; ICF awards the ACTC credential separately.

### Email Classification Taxonomy (MrGru Labels)

The following labels are used by the email classification agent (MrGru) to categorize incoming emails:

#### Primary Categories
- **LEAD** — New prospect inquiry about programs or services
- **STUDENT** — Current student communication (enrolled in a program)
- **ALUMNI** — Graduate/past student communication
- **PARTNER** — Business partner, affiliate, or collaboration inquiry
- **VENDOR** — Vendor, supplier, or service provider communication
- **SPAM** — Unsolicited commercial email, marketing spam
- **INTERNAL** — Internal team communication
- **PERSONAL** — Personal (non-business) communication to Cherie/team

#### Sub-Categories (LEAD)
- **LEAD/HOT** — High-intent prospect (asking about enrollment, pricing, next cohort)
- **LEAD/WARM** — Moderate interest (asking general questions, downloaded resources)
- **LEAD/COLD** — Low intent (general inquiry, information gathering)
- **LEAD/RETURNING** — Previous lead re-engaging

#### Sub-Categories (STUDENT)
- **STUDENT/ACTIVE** — Currently enrolled and participating
- **STUDENT/SUPPORT** — Requesting help, having issues
- **STUDENT/ADMIN** — Administrative requests (scheduling, payments, documents)

#### Additional Taxonomy Labels
- **MrGru/lead/declined** — Explicit opt-out, unsubscribe, or "not interested" reply from a lead or prospect. Auto-archive. When a lead sends a clear decline, suppress all future automated outreach to that email address.
- **MrGru/association/event** — Correspondence from ICF, EMCC, Scrum Alliance, or other professional associations about events, conferences, proposals, or membership. Hive: cherie+alex, priority 1.

- **URGENT** — Requires immediate attention
- **FOLLOWUP** — Requires follow-up but not urgent

- **HANDOFF** — Needs to be routed to a specific team member
