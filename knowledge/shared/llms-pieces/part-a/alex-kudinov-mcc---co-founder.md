## Alex Kudinov

Managing Partner

MCC

 [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexkudinov)

> “I help leaders identify the dots that, once connected, drive real change.”

Alex Kudinov is an ICF Master Certified Coach, Enterprise Coach, and co-author of Enterprise Agile Coaching. With 30+ years spanning hands-on software engineering to VP-level leadership at Fortune 500 companies, he coaches executives at the intersection of technology, business, and human potential.

Coaching Approach

### How we work together

01

#### When clients reach out

- They are navigating complex organizational change and need clarity on the best path forward.
- They feel stuck in their leadership growth and are looking for ways to break through barriers.
- They need an objective, skilled thought partner to help them make high-stakes decisions.
- They want to improve strategic alignment across teams and business units.
- They are leading transformations and need to sharpen their leadership effectiveness.

02

#### What they need most

- A structured, results-oriented approach to leadership and organizational growth.
- A confidential, professional coaching relationship that challenges assumptions and expands perspectives.
- A coach who can help them uncover key insights and leverage them for meaningful impact.
- A partner who brings experience in agile leadership, executive coaching, and business strategy.

03

#### How Alex helps

- Work with leaders to assess challenges, define success, and identify key factors that drive change.
- Facilitate structured conversations that challenge assumptions and expand problem-solving capacity.
- Provide a strategic coaching approach rooted in Agile, leadership, and business transformation principles.
- Help executives and teams refine their leadership approach, improve team dynamics, and drive lasting change.
