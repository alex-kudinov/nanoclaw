## Cherie Silas

Founder and CEO

MCCCEC

 [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cheriesilas/)

> “My passion is helping managers and leaders develop more strategic, innovative and effective organizations. I partner with them to identify and resolve the people, process and leadership problems preventing their success.”

Cherie Silas is an ICF Master Certified Coach, Certified Enterprise Coach, and co-author of Enterprise Agile Coaching. With 15 years leading organizations from Director to CEO, she now partners with executives to solve the people, process, and leadership challenges preventing their success. Her mission: to leave you better than she found you.

Coaching Approach

### How we work together

01

#### When clients reach out

- They are stressed and it’s impacting their sleep and personal lives.
- They are experiencing leadership and cultural challenges.
- They know where they want to go but can’t seem to get there.
- They have tons of projects and not enough people and money to get the work done.
- They often have delivery and quality challenges and can’t seem to find a way forward.

02

#### What they need most

- Help to make their organizations more effective and efficient.
- A confidential, neutral third party to help them think through challenges without judgment.
- Someone who will tell them the hard truths that no one else will verbalize.
- A partner who understands the pressures they face and will support them through the change and improvement process.

03

#### How Cherie helps

- Assess their organization with them to identify which people, process, culture, and leadership challenges are hindering their success.
- Partner with them to design a measurable improvement strategy.
- Help them identify and apply innovative solutions to increase organizational effectiveness so they can sleep at night and enjoy their days off!
