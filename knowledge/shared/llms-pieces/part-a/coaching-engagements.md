What We Do

### What Is a Coaching Engagement?

A coaching engagement is a structured development partnership between a credentialed coach and a leader, team, or cohort. It is not consulting, not therapy, and not a one-off workshop. It is a sustained, goal-driven process that creates lasting behavior change.

Every Tandem engagement follows our ASPIRE® framework: clear goals, validated assessments at start and end, regular coaching sessions, and measurable outcomes that both the participant and the organization can see.

We work with Fortune 500 enterprises and mid-size companies alike. The engagement structure adapts to your context, but the rigor stays the same.

Our Process

### The ASPIRE Coaching Framework

From first conversation to sustained impact, every engagement follows six structured phases.

A

#### Assess

Validated assessments - ProfileXT, Genos EQ, 360 feedback, LEAD Now! - create a clear baseline. You see where you are, not where you think you are.

Weeks 1–3

S

#### Set Goals

Coach, coachee, and organizational sponsor align on 3–5 measurable development goals tied to business outcomes. Everyone agrees on what success looks like.

Week 3–4

P

#### Partner

We match you with a coach based on industry experience, leadership level, and development focus. If the fit isn’t right, you can request a change - no friction.

Week 4

I

#### Implement

Bi-weekly coaching sessions focused on real challenges, not theory. Each session builds on the last with structured reflection and accountable action steps.

Months 2–10 (ongoing)

R

#### Review

Mid-engagement check-in with stakeholders to assess progress, recalibrate goals if needed, and ensure the coaching is delivering measurable value.

Month 5–6

E

#### Evaluate

Reassessment at engagement close measures growth against the baseline. Both the leader and the organization see concrete, documented results.

Final month

Assessment Tools

### Data-Driven Coaching Starts Here

We use validated instruments - not guesswork. Each tool measures something different, giving your coach and your organization a complete picture.

#### ProfileXT

Talent Alignment

Measures thinking style, behavioral traits, and interests against the demands of a specific role. Identifies where a leader’s natural strengths align - and where friction occurs.

Online assessment · 60 minutes · Individual report + debrief

#### Genos Emotional Intelligence

EQ Assessment

Measures how effectively a leader demonstrates emotionally intelligent workplace behavior - self-awareness, empathy, and emotional reasoning under pressure.

Online assessment · 20 minutes · Self + rater comparison report

#### 360-Degree Feedback

Multi-Rater Survey

Collects confidential feedback from direct reports, peers, and supervisors. Reveals the gap between self-perception and how others experience a leader’s behavior.

Survey-based · 5–10 raters · Aggregate report + coaching debrief

#### LEAD Now!

Leadership Effectiveness

Evaluates 21 leadership dimensions across four quadrants: creating the climate, inspiring others, building talent, and getting results. Pinpoints specific areas for development.

Online assessment · 30 minutes · Development plan output

The specific combination of assessments is tailored to each engagement. Your coach recommends the right tools based on your development goals and organizational context.

FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long is a typical coaching engagement?**

Most 1-on-1 executive coaching engagements run 6 to 12 months with bi-weekly sessions. Team coaching runs 8 months. Leadership development programs run 9 months. Focused engagements for specific transitions can be as short as 3 months.

**Q: How much does executive coaching cost?**

Investment varies based on the engagement model, duration, number of participants, and assessment tools included. We provide a detailed proposal after the discovery conversation so there are no surprises. For a deeper look at pricing factors, see our [guide to executive coaching costs](/executive-coaching-cost/).

**Q: Can engagements be customized for our organization?**

Yes. While our engagements follow proven structures, we tailor the assessment tools, session frequency, deliverables, and timeline to your organization’s specific challenges and culture. We have worked with Fortune 500 enterprises and mid-size companies alike.

**Q: What assessments do you use?**

We use validated tools including ProfileXT for talent alignment, Genos EQ for emotional intelligence, 360-degree feedback surveys, and the LEAD Now! assessment for leadership effectiveness. The specific combination depends on the engagement goals.

**Q: How do you match coaches to clients?**

We match based on industry experience, leadership level, and development goals. Both of our MCCs have corporate leadership backgrounds, and our team of 9 credentialed coaches provides depth across specialties. Clients always have the option to request a different coach.

**Q: What happens during the discovery call?**

A 30-minute conversation to understand your goals, the leaders you want to develop, and the outcomes you are looking for. We share how our engagements work and recommend a path forward - or tell you honestly if we are not the right fit. No pressure, no sales pitch.
