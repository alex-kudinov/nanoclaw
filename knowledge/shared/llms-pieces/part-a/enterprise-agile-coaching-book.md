## Enterprise Agile Coaching

Sustaining Organizational Change Through the Invitational Approach

By **Cherie Silas, MCC** • **Alex Kudinov, MCC** • **Michael De La Maza, PhD**

[Get Your Copy on Amazon](https://lnk.tco.ac/amzn)

Most agile transformations fail when the coach leaves. This book changes that.

Compliance-driven change is easy to install—but it withers when the forcing mechanism disappears. Organizations invest heavily in agile transformations, only to watch them roll back to the original state within months of the coach’s departure.

**Sustainable transformation requires a different strategy.** This book introduces the Invitational Approach to enterprise agile coaching—a methodology that puts the client in the seat of responsibility from day one.

The result? Changes that stick because they’re owned, not installed. Business agility that survives leadership transitions, market shifts, and the inevitable pressures to “go back to how things were.”
