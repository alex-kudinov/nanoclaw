Common questions

### Frequently asked questions

**Q: Which course covers which credential level?**

Each instrument matches a credential level:

- **ACC level:** ACC BARS Training ($49)
- **PCC level:** PCC Markers Training ($99)
- **MCC level:** MCC BARS Training ($49)

Take the course for each credential level you want to learn the instrument for.

**Q: Do I need all three courses?**

Only if you want MCS at all three credential levels. Most coaches pursue MCS at the level matching their current ICF credential. If you hold a PCC and want to qualify as a PCC-level mentor coach, you need only the PCC Markers course.

The Complete Bundle ($149) is designed for coaches who want comprehensive instrument knowledge across all levels - either because they plan to mentor at multiple levels or because they want the fullest understanding of how ICF evaluates coaching.

**Q: What do these courses actually teach?**

Tandem's courses are independent instructor-led video programs. You watch two MCC-level coaches walk through each BARS statement and PCC Marker, analyze real coaching recordings, complete scored assessments, and download working tools.

They build your skill with the instruments ICF uses to evaluate coaching. They are professional development and are not a replacement for any training ICF requires or offers.

**Q: Do these courses satisfy ICF Mentor Coach Specialization requirements?**

No. These are independent professional development courses. ICF has not reviewed or approved them, and they do not satisfy the evaluation training requirement for the Mentor Coach Specialization.

ICF determines what training meets MCS requirements. Confirm current requirements and approved options directly with ICF before relying on any course for your application.

**Q: Are these courses approved by or affiliated with ICF?**

No. These courses are not ICF-approved, ICF-accredited, or endorsed by the International Coaching Federation, and Tandem Coaching is not affiliated with ICF. ICF has not reviewed or approved them.

Each course issues a Tandem Coaching Academy Certificate of Completion documenting the training you completed. It carries no ICF name, logo, or mark. For the requirements of the Mentor Coach Specialization, consult ICF directly.

**Q: How long do I have access?**

Lifetime access. All video lessons, downloadable tools, and course materials remain available to you indefinitely. Revisit any lesson whenever you need to refresh your understanding of an instrument.

**Q: What platform are the courses on?**

All courses are hosted on the Tandem Coaching Academy at [community.tandemcoaching.academy](https://community.tandemcoaching.academy). You receive access credentials immediately after purchase and can start right away.

**Q: Is there a money-back guarantee?**

Yes. If the course isn't what you expected, email [hello@tandemcoach.co](mailto:hello@tandemcoach.co) within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. Refunds are not available after your completion certificate has been issued.
