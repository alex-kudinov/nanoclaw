##### These dates only cover part of your hours – here’s the rest

Each package has two parts. The *group sessions* – 8 hours, four 2-hour sessions – run on the cohort dates listed above. The *individual sessions* – 3 hours, two 90-minute sessions – you schedule directly with your mentor coach at times that suit you, so they never appear on this calendar.

That’s why the listed dates look shorter than the full engagement. A group cohort runs about five weeks; your individual sessions are what carry it the rest of the way.

##### Important: ICF requires 3 months, and tracking that is on you

ICF requires mentor coaching to span *a minimum of 3 months* from your first session to your last. We don’t gate your scheduling – so if you book both individual sessions right after the group cohort ends, your engagement spans about six weeks and *ICF will reject the submission.* You’d have to do mentor coaching again.

Note the date of your first group session, and schedule your final individual session at least three months after it. ICF also requires a minimum of 3 individual hours, which is why there’s no all-group option at any price.

**Q: ACC Renewal Requirements (Full Details)**

From *ICF Credential Renewal Guide* (rev. 2025.11.03):

For ACC recertification, the following is required:

- At least 40 Continuing Coach Education (CCE) credits in 3 years
- Of the 40 CCE credits:
  - At least 24 must be in Core Competencies
  - At least 3 must be in coaching ethics
  - **10 hours of Mentor Coaching** with an eligible mentor coach over minimum 3 months

The 10 hours of mentor coaching count toward the 24 Core Competency CCEs.

**Important:** ACC renewal requires the same group/individual split as initial credentialing: at least 3 hours must be individual mentor coaching.

**Eligible mentor coaches:** Must hold ACC (recertified at least once), PCC, or MCC. Tandem’s mentors are MCCs.

**Q: Initial ACC/PCC/MCC Requirements**

For initial credentialing at any level, ICF requires:

- 10 hours total mentor coaching
- Maximum 7 hours may be group
- Minimum 3 hours must be individual (1-on-1)
- Completed over at least 3 months

This applies to:

- ACC via Level 1 Path or Portfolio Path
- PCC via Level 2 Path or Portfolio Path
- MCC via Level 3 Path or Portfolio Path

**Note:** If you’re enrolled in a Tandem ACC, Systems Coach (PCC bridge), or Professional Coach program, mentor coaching is already included in your program.

**Q: PCC and MCC Renewal**

PCC and MCC renewals do **not** require mentor coaching.

However, you can use mentor coaching toward your CCE requirements:

- Up to 10 hours of mentor coaching (giving or receiving) counts toward the 40 CCE credits
- Counts as Core Competency credits

If you’re a PCC or MCC looking to fulfill CCE requirements, mentor coaching is an excellent option for skill development while earning credits.

**Q: What Counts as Mentor Coaching?**

ICF defines mentor coaching as:

> “A process where a coach receives guidance and feedback from a more experienced coach to enhance their coaching skills and performance.”

For a fuller primer on the practice itself, see our guide to [what ICF mentor coaching involves](/icf-mentor-coaching/).

**Key elements:**

- Must be with an ICF-credentialed mentor coach (ACC recertified, PCC, or MCC)
- Must include observation and feedback on your coaching
- Can be group or individual format
- Must span at least 3 months

**What doesn’t count:**

- General coaching skills training (that’s CCE, not mentor coaching)
- Coaching supervision (separate category)
- Peer coaching without a credentialed mentor

**Q: Timeline Requirements**

ICF requires mentor coaching to occur over a **minimum of 3 months**.

**Example:** If your first session is March 14, your last session must be June 14 or later.

This applies to both:

- Initial credentialing (ACC, PCC, MCC)
- ACC renewal

Plan accordingly when scheduling your mentor coaching. Our group sessions run on a schedule designed to meet this requirement.

**Q: Documentation for ICF Application**

When you complete mentor coaching with Tandem, you’ll receive:

- Certificate of completion
- Mentor coach name and credentials
- Start and end dates of engagement
- Total hours completed

This documentation is exactly what ICF requires for your credential application or renewal submission.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What’s the difference between group and individual mentor coaching?**

**Group mentor coaching:** Learn alongside other coaches, observe peer coaching demonstrations, and receive feedback in a collaborative setting. Great for exposure to diverse coaching styles and peer learning.

**Individual mentor coaching:** 1-on-1 attention with your MCC mentor. Personalized feedback on your specific coaching, flexible scheduling, and deeper exploration of your development areas.

ICF allows both formats to count toward mentor coaching requirements. The key difference is the level of personalized attention and flexibility.

Every package can be paid in full or in 3 monthly installments.

Every package exceeds ICF’s 10-hour minimum and includes the completion documentation you submit with your credential application. Because each mentor coach is a Master Certified Coach *and* an ICF credential assessor, you’re receiving feedback from someone who evaluates real credential submissions rather than merely meeting an hours requirement.

**Q: Is mentor coaching the same as coaching supervision?**

**No.** ICF treats mentor coaching and [coaching supervision](/coaching-supervision-vs-coaching-mentoring/) as separate categories.

**Mentor coaching** gives you feedback on your coaching against the ICF Core Competencies to earn or renew a credential. It is competency- and evaluation-focused, and it is what ICF requires for credentialing.

**Coaching supervision** is ongoing reflective practice on your work as a coach: your cases, ethics, and development. It is valuable, but it does *not* satisfy the ICF mentor coaching requirement. If your goal is a credential or renewal, mentor coaching is what you need.

**Q: Do I need individual mentor coaching for ACC renewal?**

**Yes.** ICF requires 10 hours of mentor coaching for ACC renewal, with at least 3 hours delivered as individual (1-on-1) sessions.

This is the same group/individual split required for all ICF credentials. Our ACC Renewal package includes 8 group hours and 3 individual hours, exceeding the minimum.
