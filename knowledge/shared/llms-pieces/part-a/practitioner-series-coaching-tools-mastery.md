### What changes in your sessions after this course

Six practical shifts. Each is something you'll do differently in your next session.

01

#### Read whether a tool fits this moment

Run the five-move discernment loop before reaching for a worksheet - and recognise the moments when staying with what's alive beats any tool.

02

#### Name the shift you intend

Co-name the intended shift with the client - awareness, clarity, action, integration - before picking the instrument. The conversation changes when the purpose has a name.

03

#### Match a tool on four dimensions

Purpose, format, time, readiness. A repeatable check you can run in real time, not a memorised tool list.

04

#### Design and repair a tool-chain

Sequence tools across sessions toward client growth - and diagnose what to do when a chain breaks mid-engagement.

05

#### Hold presence while tools are running

Tools as vehicles for the client's insight, never a substitute for your presence. The mind behind the tool stays the point.

06

#### Walk away with your own Tool-Selection Map

A personal decision aid you can pull up in a session, tuned to the niches you actually coach. Tested against unseen scenarios in the final module.

**Q: 01 The Discernment Mindset Read the Moment - the prelude 2.5h · 5 lessons · CC 2.5**

Why a tool in the wrong hands is dangerous, and what changes when discernment leads. The mindset the rest of the course builds on.

- Right Tool, Right Mind - the ethos
- Collection vs Mastery - why knowing 400 tools isn't the skill
- The five moves - map of the course
- The cost of over-tooling - tools as avoidance
- Staying with what's alive - when the moment needs you, not a worksheet

**Q: 02 Read the Moment Where in the arc, and what's alive now 3.0h · 5 lessons · CC 2.5 / RD 0.5**

Before you choose a tool, you read the moment. The engagement arc - discovery, goal-setting, action, reflection, review - and the signals that tell you a tool fits or doesn't.

- The five phases of an engagement arc
- Where tools live in each phase, and the classic phase-mismatch error
- Signals a tool will help - stuckness, abstraction, overwhelm
- Signals to wait - emotion, fresh insight, trust-building

**Q: 03 Name the Purpose & Match the Tool The heart of selection 4.0h · 7 lessons · CC 2.5 / RD 1.5**

Naming the intended shift, then matching a tool to it on four dimensions. Includes how to use a 498-tool library as a selection lens instead of a catalog to memorise.

- The four intents - awareness, clarity, action, integration
- Co-naming the purpose with the client (Establishes Agreements)
- The four fit dimensions - purpose, format, time, readiness
- Choosing between two near-equivalent tools in the moment
- Using the catalog's produces/consumes metadata as a search lens

**Q: 04 The Craft of Tool-Chains Design, extend, prune, repair 5.0h · 8 lessons · CC 1.5 / RD 3.5**

How to sequence tools across sessions - grounded in adult-learning theory (Kolb's experiential cycle, Vygotsky's zone of proximal development). Including how to recognise and repair a chain that's breaking.

- Holding a chain as a hypothesis, not a script (presence over plan)
- Chain design principles - length, extension, pruning
- Constructing a chain toward a specific growth outcome
- Diagnosing break-shapes and repairing mid-engagement

**Q: 05 Tool-Chains in Context 10+ worked chains across niches 4.5h · 13 lessons · CC 3.0 / RD 1.5**

Self-paced by niche. The five moves applied to real client scenarios across life, career, executive, and ADHD coaching. Pick the chains that match your practice.

- Life coaching - clarity, integration, habit-formation chains
- Career coaching - decision, transition, identity chains
- Executive coaching - presence, stakeholder, leadership chains
- ADHD coaching - executive function, energy, systems chains

**Q: 06 Build Your Tool-Selection Map The capstone artifact 2.0h · 3 lessons · CC 2.0**

Synthesize the method into a personal decision aid you can pull up in a session. Tested against unseen scenarios. The artifact you keep using long after the course ends.

- Translating the five moves into a one-page personal map
- Testing the map on unseen client scenarios
- The practicum - a complete session-plan you can defend on competency grounds

**Q: What's the framework, in plain English?**

Right Tool, Right Mind. Five moves you run in the moment: *Read the Moment* (is a tool called for at all?), *Name the Purpose* (what shift am I after?), *Match the Tool* (four-dimension fit check), *Chain the Sequence* (sequencing across sessions), *Hold Presence* (the tool serves presence, never substitutes for it).

The "mind" is the point. A tool in the wrong hands is dangerous. The course teaches the hand.
