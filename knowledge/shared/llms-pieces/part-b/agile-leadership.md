### How do I deal with difficult stakeholders?

Roman Pichler addressed stakeholder management directly in the Best Agile Articles 2018 workshop. The article frames stakeholder challenges through intersecting lenses: psychological safety, conditions that enable risk-taking, and organizational hierarchy dynamics. Dealing with difficult stakeholders requires understanding these dimensions together, not treating stakeholder friction as an isolated interpersonal problem.

#### Key Takeaways

- Stakeholder management sits at the crossroads of change leadership, psychological safety, and organizational culture — not a standalone skill.
- Agile wisdom spreads faster when practitioners share it directly, not just through books or frameworks.
- Distributed teams, sprint decisions, and budgeting challenges all connect back to how well leaders manage relationships above and below them.
- The best agile thinking in any given year deserves curation — practitioners shouldn't have to hunt across hundreds of sources to find signal.
