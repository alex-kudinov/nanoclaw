### Career Anchor Sort
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/career-anchor-sort/

URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/career-branding-workbook/
When to use: A client wants to get intentional about how they're perceived professionally | A client is preparing for a job search and needs to articulate their unique value | A client wants to build visibility and credibility in their field
