When to use: Client has stated a goal but hasn't examined what's actually motivating it | Client wants to identify the specific habits that will accelerate or derail progress on a near-term goal | Coach wants to move from goal identification to a concrete analysis of enabling conditions and risks
Format: worksheet | Time: 15 min | Moment: mid-session
Produces: stated short-term goal with genuine motivation named, enabling habits list with triggers or contexts, risk habits list that could derail progress
Coaching scenarios (3):
- The client setting goals as performance without examining what is actually driving them
- The client who names the goal but has never examined the habits that will support or undermine it
- The client articulating a goal for the first time who needs to test whether it is theirs
Pairs with: Reading Log, Goal Achievement Planner, Session Rating Template, Session Rating Template

### WOOP Goal Exercise
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/woop-goal-exercise/
When to use: A client who sets ambitious goals but hasn't planned for the internal obstacles that consistently derail them | Someone who responds well to research-backed frameworks and wants a structured approach to goal pursuit | A professional who's motivated to start something new but needs to bridge intention and follow-through
Format: worksheet | Time: 15 min | Moment: mid-session
Produces: wish, outcome, obstacle, and if-then plan, named specific internal obstacle to goal progress, written if-then implementation intention statement, scheduled check-in date with obstacle review
Requires: specific goal with genuine stakes, honest self-knowledge of recurring derailment patterns
Coaching scenarios (3):
- The client who sets ambitious goals and is repeatedly surprised when internal obstacles derail them
- The client who responds well to research-backed frameworks and wants the most reliable goal-setting structure available
- The client motivated to start but whose initial momentum consistently dissolves before the goal is achieved
Pairs with: Annual Goal Tracker, Celebration and Wins Log, Celebrate Your Wins Journal, Debt Payoff Tracker

### WOOP Goal Setting
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/woop-goal-setting/
When to use: Client keeps setting goals but stalls when obstacles appear | Client skips from wishing to planning without honestly naming what gets in the way | Client wants a goal framework that forces realistic assessment before commitment
Format: worksheet | Time: 15 min | Moment: mid-session

Requires: specific goal candidate ready for obstacle mapping
Coaching scenarios (3):
- The client who selects a safe version of the goal rather than the real one
- The client who treats the Plan as the primary step and rushes through W, O, and O to reach it
- The client applying WOOP to a goal they have already committed to and partially attempted
Pairs with: Barrier Resolution Planner, Growth Mindset Action Plan, Goal Obstacles Planner, Growth Mindset

### Wheel of Life Goals
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/wheel-of-life-goals/
When to use: Client's goals are heavily concentrated in career and finances with other domains largely unexamined | Client wants to see their priorities across all life areas before deciding where to focus | Coach wants to use the Wheel of Life framework to prompt goal-setting across eight domains simultaneously
Format: worksheet | Time: 30 min | Moment: opener
Produces: goal statement per each of eight life areas, visible goal concentration pattern across domains, identified underinvested life areas for redirection
Requires: willingness to assess all life areas simultaneously
Coaching scenarios (3):
- The client whose goals cluster in two or three areas while the rest are blank or sparse
- The client who wants to see priorities across life areas but is uncertain where to focus
- The client doing a quarterly review who wants to check alignment across all eight life areas
Pairs with: Values vs. Actions Audit, Annual Goals Planner, Life Goals by Category, Life Update
