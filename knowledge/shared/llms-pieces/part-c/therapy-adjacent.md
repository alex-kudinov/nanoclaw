## Therapeutic Support (9 tools)

### DBT DEAR MAN Skill
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/dbt-dear-man-skill/
When to use: A client needs to make a specific request or say no and wants to do it effectively | Someone preparing for a high-stakes conversation where the outcome genuinely matters | Walking through Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce before a difficult ask
Format: worksheet | Time: 30 min | Moment: mid-session
Produces: scripted direct request or refusal statement, reinforcement framing naming benefit to other party, negotiation range map for the conversation
Requires: named specific interpersonal situation with concrete ask
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Manager who hints instead of asking and then resents the outcome
- Leader who goes emotionally hard in conflict and loses the outcome
- New director needing to say no to her own manager for the first time
- Client with chronic boundary erosion who can't hold a negotiated agreement
Pairs with: Communication Quality Checklist, Team Engagement Planner, Johari Window, Emotional Vocabulary Reference

### DBT Distress Tolerance: TIPP Skills
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/dbt-distress-tolerance-tipp-skills/
When to use: A client gets flooded by intense emotion and needs physiological tools to come down quickly | Someone learning Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, and Paired muscle relaxation | Tracking which TIPP skills they actually use across a week and how effective each one is
Format: tracker | Time: 15 min | Moment: opener
Produces: 7-day TIPP skill usage log, effectiveness rating per crisis situation, personal best-fit physiological skill identification
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Executive who cannot come down after high-stakes presentations
- Manager with anger that escalates faster than she can catch it
- Client in burnout recovery building physiological regulation skills
- High performer whose only stress regulation strategy is exercise
Pairs with: Emotional Regulation Zones, Coping Strategy Playlist, Mindfulness and Stress Management Toolkit, Stress Management Plan

### DBT Emotion Regulation Check-In
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/dbt-emotion-regulation-check-in/
When to use: A client wants a structured way to name and respond to emotions rather than just reacting | Someone identifying emotion, intensity, vulnerability factors, and opposite action in sequence | Selecting the right DBT skill tier based on what they're actually experiencing right now
Format: worksheet | Time: 15 min | Moment: opener
Produces: named emotion with intensity rating 0-10, vulnerability factor score with contributing factors, selected opposite action for the primary emotion, post-skill reflection on what helped and what changed
Requires: basic emotion vocabulary for naming feelings, familiarity with TIPP skills for high-intensity routing
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Director who acts on emotion and regrets it within the hour
- Client learning to distinguish anxiety from anger in high-stakes moments
- Manager who understands emotion regulation theory but cannot apply it
- Leader building a self-awareness practice after 360 feedback about emotional unpredictability
Pairs with: Trigger Awareness Log, Sitting with Feelings, Body-Emotion Connection Map, Self-Compassion Practice

### DBT FAST Skill
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/dbt-fast-skill/
When to use: A client tends to apologize, over-explain, or compromise their values to keep the peace | Someone who wants to hold their ground without damaging the relationship | Practicing Fair, No Apologies, Stick to Values, and Truthful in a real situation
Format: worksheet | Time: 15 min | Moment: mid-session
Produces: written fairness assessment for both parties, map of reflexive versus genuine apologies in the situation, named values at stake and cost of compromise, post-interaction self-respect review
Requires: named specific situation where self-respect eroded
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Manager who apologizes reflexively and then resents having done it
- VP who compromises values in executive team conversations to maintain standing
- Client rebuilding self-respect after a manipulative working relationship
- High-achiever who tells partial truths to manage others' expectations
Pairs with: Johari Window, Emotional Vocabulary Reference, Social Media Relationship Assessment, Feedback Reflection Worksheet

- New manager having first difficult conversations with former peers
- Executive whose family reports she brings the same edge home

### DBT Wise Mind Worksheet
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/dbt-wise-mind-worksheet/
When to use: A client makes decisions from pure emotion or pure logic and misses what the other side knows | Someone learning to locate the overlap between reasonable mind and emotional mind | Building a daily practice of pausing to access wise mind before responding
Format: worksheet | Time: 30 min | Moment: mid-session
Produces: Venn diagram with fact-based and emotion-based inputs, synthesized Wise Mind statement and decision direction, practice tracker of Wise Mind consultations over time
Requires: named specific decision or stuck situation to work through
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Analytical executive who dismisses emotional data in consequential decisions
- Client paralyzed by emotion in a decision that requires action
- Leader who acts on emotion quickly and accurately but cannot explain why to others
- Client in a values conflict between career advancement and personal life demands
Pairs with: Trigger Awareness Log, ABC Model, Wheel of Emotions, Self-Awareness and Confidence Scale

### Panic Assessment
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/panic-assessment/
When to use: A client documenting a panic episode while details are still fresh and accurate | Someone tracking symptoms, context, and behavioral changes after an acute anxiety event | Capturing what happened before, during, and after a panic attack to find patterns over time
Format: assessment | Time: 15 min | Moment: opener
Produces: timestamped panic episode record with pre-attack context, symptom checklist for the specific episode, distress and anticipatory worry severity ratings, documented behavioral avoidance changes post-episode
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Executive who dismisses recurring physical symptoms as work stress
- Client using busyness to avoid addressing recurrent panic episodes
- Leader who has sought medical support and is using the assessment as a coaching adjunct
- Client who uses the assessment to intellectualize rather than feel
Pairs with: ADHD Thought Reflection Worksheet, ADHD Gratitude Practice, Self-Compassion Practice, Trigger Awareness Log

### Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10)
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/perceived-stress-scale-pss-10/
When to use: A client wants an objective read on how much stress they're actually carrying right now | Using a validated 10-item scale to measure perceived stress over the past month | Understanding whether the strain is about the load itself or the sense of losing control over it
Format: assessment | Time: 5 min | Moment: between-sessions
Produces: PSS-10 total score with low/moderate/high category, coping capacity sub-score from reverse-scored items, month-over-month stress baseline for tracking
Coaching scenarios (4):
- High-performer who normalizes elevated stress as the cost of ambition
- Leader under acute organizational pressure seeking to gauge his own baseline
- Client who has taken the PSS before and uses scores to track her coaching progress
- Coaching client referred by HR following a performance conversation
Pairs with: Weekly Check-In, Self-Care Assessment, Emotional Regulation Zones, Coping Strategy Playlist

### Window of Tolerance Worksheet
URL: https://tandemcoach.co/coaching-tools/window-of-tolerance-worksheet/
When to use: A client goes reactive under pressure and wants to understand their nervous system better | Someone identifying whether they tend to go up into hyperarousal or down into shutdown | Building a personal regulation toolkit matched to their specific direction of dysregulation
Format: worksheet | Time: 30 min | Moment: mid-session
Produces: personal hyperarousal and hypoarousal symptom profile, identified default dysregulation direction under stress, window-shrinking trigger list with personal specifics, matched regulation strategy card for each zone
Coaching scenarios (4):
- Leader who reads all stress as a performance signal and cannot distinguish zones
- Director in burnout recovery who has been operating in hypoarousal for months
- Client who oscillates rapidly between hyperarousal and hypoarousal within single meetings
- Coach introducing the model to a client as a shared language for ongoing work
Pairs with: Emotional Regulation Zones, Stress Management Plan, DBT Distress Tolerance: TIPP Skills, Coping Strategy Playlist
